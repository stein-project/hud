# HUD 0.1.12: the 1% low without the pause menu

The performance element now shows only the game's own frames, so closing the Esc menu no longer looks like lag.

- **1% low and peak without the pause menu**: on the Esc menu (where the game also goes when its window loses focus) the game holds the frame rate down on purpose, and those slow frames used to drag the 1% low and the peak down for a few seconds after you went back. They no longer count.
- **Numbers right away**: back in the game, the 1% low and the peak update at once, without waiting a second with the menu's numbers.
