# HUD 0.1.17: the minimap and the target in mods of their own

The HUD keeps only what fits any server. The minimap became a separate mod, and the target moved to Combat.

- **Minimap on its own**: to keep using it, add the Minimap mod in the Stein Client. Its place on the screen and its options are kept.
- **Target in Combat**: the target element now comes with Combat, in the same place on the screen and with the same options.
- **Distance in the Tab list removed**: the column, the "nearby" filter and sorting by distance left the Tab list. Whoever used one of them goes back to the default.
- **Update notice**: the Loader's Panel shows when a new version of this mod is out.
