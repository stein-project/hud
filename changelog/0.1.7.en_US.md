# The Tab list and minimap in the colours you chose

The HUD now learns who is an ally or an enemy from Stein Loader, and paints them in the colours you chose.

- **Tab list headers in your colours**: the clan, ally and enemy groups in the Tab list use the colours chosen in Combat instead of fixed ones.
- **Allies and enemies through Stein Loader**: the HUD asks the Loader who is who — it works with Combat and with any other mod that says so.
- **Lighter Tab on crowded servers**: the Tab list and the heads no longer look players up one by one through the whole list — with hundreds of people in the Tab, that was a search per row every frame.
- **Under the hood**: the HUD no longer talks to Minecraft directly — items, effects, map, Tab and drawing go through Stein Loader. On screen, nothing changes.
- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.23**: update with the installer before the mod.
