# HUD 0.1.8: cleaner defaults

New default settings for new players. If you already play, your settings stay as they are.

- **Stats**: FPS only, horizontal and without a background.
- **Armor**: also shows the durability of the item in your hand.
- **Direction**: strip without a background.
- **Inventory**: empty slots are hidden.
- **Tab**: your own name shows in the list.
