# HUD 0.1.9: standard background and Tab

Requires Stein Loader 0.1.25.

- **Background**: stats, direction, effects, inventory and armor use the grid's standard background, with a **Background transparency** option. Their own background options were removed; if you had the stats background on, pick the transparency again.
- **Inventory**: the old background option is now called **Slot marks**.
- **Tab**: new **Background transparency**, **Row background** and **Font** (Stein or game) options. The Tab now uses the rounded Stein background.
